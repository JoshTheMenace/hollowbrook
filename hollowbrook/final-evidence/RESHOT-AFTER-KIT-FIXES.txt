RESHOT AFTER THE LEDGER-C KIT FIXES (609b2a5)
=============================================
The three fixes under test:
  1. brazier()      — the ember and core cones were cyl(rTop, rBottom) written
                      the wrong way round, so the fire was a funnel standing on
                      its point.  Now wide at the coals, narrow at the sky.
  2. bracketLantern()/postLantern() — the pane is its own switchable mesh and
                      the pool is always built (hidden when dark).  The RENDER
                      is supposed to be unchanged; that is asserted below.
  3. barricade({ kind: 'carts' }) — wheel rim and spokes go oakSilver with a
                      dark hub disc proud of them.

HOW THESE WERE TAKEN
  Dev server on port 5223.  Headless system Chrome via puppeteer-core
  (--use-angle=metal --enable-gpu), driving window.__shot in the page.
  Every capture below is `?only=<district>&polish=off` at the frame's own
  committed pixel size — established by re-shooting keephill's
  vista-from-the-keep both ways and diffing against the committed file:
  with the polish layer ON the frame gained pale cloud bands the committed
  frame does not have; with `polish=off` it matched the committed frame
  everywhere except the brazier.  So the committed evidence was captured
  without src/polish.js and the replacements are too.

  Every candidate was diffed against its committed file pixel-for-pixel
  (threshold 24/765 on RGB sum) so the change could be attributed before
  anything was overwritten.

CONTAMINATION — READ THIS BEFORE TRUSTING ANY NEW FRAME
--------------------------------------------------------
Other agents were editing the tree while these were captured.  At capture
time `git status` showed uncommitted modifications to src/districts/
{chapelclose,keephill,marketlow,millreach,southgate}.js, src/main.js,
src/scene.js and src/game/*, plus two untracked new files (src/polish.js,
src/game/daynight.js).  Consequences that are visible in the frames:

  - every district has gained a new UNLIT `relight-brazier` (an integration
    point for the game layer).  It is what the southgate, chapelclose and
    keephill gate-axis diffs above actually are.
  - keephill has gained a lean-to on the skyline, a changed keep-tower
    silhouette and two window slits in the hall's east wall.  Those are in
    the replaced ward-from-walk.jpg.  They are not mine and they are not
    the kit fix.
  - src/polish.js is now imported by src/main.js, so a default-URL capture
    carries a haze/cloud layer the committed evidence does not.  Hence
    `polish=off` on everything here.

Nothing in this pass edited a source file, a gate, or city-plan.json.
