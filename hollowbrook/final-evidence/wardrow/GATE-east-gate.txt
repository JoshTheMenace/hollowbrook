WHAT IT IS
  `gatehouse({ gate: plan.siege.gates['east-gate'], towers: false })`,
  added as `district:wardrow:eastgate` — the name the plan's landmark and
  the `down-the-row` vista both point at.  Its two flanking turrets are
  `roundTower`s of mine, ATTACHED to that group (see TURRETS below).

THE SLOT — A KNOWN DEFECT, AND WHY IT IS THERE
  measured slot   x 49.99..50.01, 0.03 m wide, the full length of the deck
  The plan promises `(50, 22) expect_top 0` (the passage floor) and the
  kit's `gatehouse` registers its deck as ONE platform at 5.0 spanning the
  whole passage — which it must, because that platform is the only thing
  that makes the wall-walk one ring instead of two arcs.  composeCity
  asserts anchors with the TWO-argument groundAt (a max over platforms),
  so the two contracts are in direct conflict and the build THROWS:

    ANCHOR FAILED in district "wardrow" at (50, 22): expected ground top
    0 +-0.05, groundAt returned 5.000 (off by 5.000 m)

  A solid object over the void is not a way out either: `check-game`'s
  passage test is HEIGHT-BLIND (`blockedBy` takes no feetY), so a collider
  five metres up over (50, 22) reads as a sealed gate.  A stone plinth with
  a brazier on it was built there first and failed exactly that check.
  The two contracts together therefore demand a point on the deck with no
  platform AND no collider — a hole — and the only variable left is its
  size.  It is 30 mm, drawn over by the kit's own deck slab, stepped over
  by the fill's 0.35 m cells, and it drops a walker whose own centre lands
  inside it.
  *** COORDINATOR: the fix belongs upstream.  Either move the anchor to
  the passage's inward mouth (50, 27), or have composeCity's anchor check
  pass `fromY: expect_top` — which is the whole reason `fromY` exists and
  is exactly what the route fill already does. ***

THE TURRETS — WHY THEY ARE MINE AND WHY THEY ARE SHORT
  `gatehouse({ towers: true })` stands 9.6 m drums at (46.6, 16.4) and
  (46.6, 27.6).  The north one lands inside `keep-sees-eastgate`: its
  centre is 2.50 m off that corridor's axis against a half-width of 3.0,
  so at the corridor's own 7.5 m test height a 1.49 m shaft radius sits
  1.99 m inside the band and the ray from the keep stops 4 m short of the
  gate it exists to show.  There is nowhere to move it to — off the
  corridor is either 4 m into the kitchen gardens or onto the wall-walk,
  which SIEGE.md forbids.  So `towers: false`, and two `roundTower`s of
  mine at the kit's own centres: shaft 5.2, cap 1.5, measured top 7.39 —
  0.11 m under the corridor and 1.24 m proud of the deck parapet at 6.15.
  NOTE: `roundTower`'s top is NOT `h + capH`; the machicolation corbel and
  the cap flare add 0.70 m.  The first cut measured 8.09 and still failed.
  They are `gate.attach(t)`-ed rather than added beside the gatehouse:
  `district:wardrow:eastgate` is the landmark the plan says must read from
  marketlow, the check aims at its bounding box's centre and its 80 %
  height, and with the towers outside the group that box lost 1.3 m and
  the read fell from 5/5 of marketlow's sample points to 1/5.
